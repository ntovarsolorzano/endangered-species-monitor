Amur Leopard

Panthera pardus orientalis

Critically Endangered

Mammal

forest

Habitat

Amur leopards are adapted to the cool, temperate forests of the Russian Far East and northern China. They prefer areas with rugged, rocky terrain and dense vegetation which provide cover for hunting and raising cubs.

warning

Threats

The primary threats to the Amur leopard are poaching, both of leopards and their prey, habitat loss from logging and human settlement, and a critically small gene pool which makes the population vulnerable to disease.
